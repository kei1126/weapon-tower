Weapon Tower Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Weapon Tower does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Information we do not collect
We do not obtain your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other information that could identify you.

Network use
The app does not connect to the internet. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no transmission of data to any external service.

Data stored on your device
Only your unlocked level, volume settings, and language preference are saved, and only on your device. None of it leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Sharing with third parties
Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share.

Children
The app is safe for users of any age. Since no personal information is collected, no parental consent is required for any data collection.

Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
